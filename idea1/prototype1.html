Goal

        
Use Vim motions and operators (d, y, p, ., u, Ctrl+R) to remove the monsters (🦊) and place the treasure (💎) on the golden tile (🔶).

        
💎 Treasure spot: 🔶 (line 8)

      


      

        
Cheat-sheet (implemented)

        

          Motions: h j k l | w b | 0 ^ $ | f t ; , | gg G

          Operators: d, y (with motions). dd, yy (line ops).

          Other: p (put), . (repeat last change), u (undo), Ctrl+R (redo).

          Counts supported: eg. 3w, 2dw.
        

      


      

        
Controls & Tips

        
    
          
  • Type commands as in Vim — try dw to delete a word.
    • 
          
  • To find a char on the line type f then the character.
    • 
          
  • Use counts like 3j to move down 3 lines.
    • 
          
  • Press p to paste yanked text.
